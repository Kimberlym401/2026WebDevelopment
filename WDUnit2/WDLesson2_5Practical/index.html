Laika Studios Movies-

Featured below are movies that have been made by the movie company Laika. They have only made a total of 6 movies since they have spent up to 16 years just creating one. The special part about these movie is that they use stop-motion. Stop-motion is when the creators take a picture of an object or character, move it slightly, and then take another picture. They string all these images one after another to create movement of the object.
